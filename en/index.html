Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

€19, one payment. Lifetime license for your PC. You get a 7-day trial when you install.

Does it work on macOS or Linux?

No, Windows 10 and 11 only for now.

Do I need OBS for Streamer Mode?

To control it yes (official OBS WebSocket plugin). The mascot works on its own.

Is it safe to run commands?

Only what you allow gets executed, validated before launching.

Where is my configuration stored?

In your Windows profile, exportable whenever you want.

Can I use it from my phone?

Yes. In Settings turn on Mobile and tablet, open the local URL on your phone and enter the 4-digit PIN. The deck, folders and PTT run over Wi‑Fi; nothing is installed on the phone.

How do I get the app and the license?

After paying we send you the Windows installer and a THEPAD key. Paste it in Settings → License.

What is a store preset?

A ready-made set of profiles and buttons. Download it, import it in Settings → Pages, and it is added without wiping what you already have.
